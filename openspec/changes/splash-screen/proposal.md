# Proposal: Splash screen

## Intent
The installed app opens on a blank screen on iOS and on Android's generated amber splash, then cuts to whatever the first route paints. Ian wants a branded launch that keeps the icon's essence and hands off smoothly to the app. He picked design **E, "Rise and dock"** (A + C) from https://claude.ai/artifact/11LG3Pwqg4uye8eWgkgifD (2026-10-08).

## Design E
- **Launch frame.** An amber `#f2b544` full screen with the icon's dumbbell centered. It is identical to the native launch image, so the native-to-web handoff is invisible.
- **Rise.** From 0.12s the seven week bars rise Monday→Sunday, with a 55ms stagger and a slight overshoot. At 0.50s the dumbbell does one rep.
- **Contract.** At 0.95s the amber screen contracts into the icon tile (112px, radius 26), with the bars and dumbbell scaling with it. The app fades in around it.
- **Dock.** From about 1.38s the tile flies into the central tab-bar action and rounds into its circle. At about 1.78s it hands off to the real button. Total is about 1.9s.

## Scope
### In Scope
- **iOS launch images.** `apple-touch-startup-image` PNGs for current iPhone portrait sizes, rendered from one source SVG of the launch frame. The `<link>` tags go in the root layout.
- **Splash overlay.** It is painted from the server HTML, so the first web frame is already amber. It runs only in the installed app (`display-mode: standalone`) and once per cold load, never on client navigations. The browser tab gets no splash.
- **Dock target.** The overlay docks into the live central action: the start +, or the back-to-workout button while a workout is open. With no tab bar (sign-in, register, /workout), the tile scales and fades out in place.
- **Reduced motion.** Under `prefers-reduced-motion` there is no rise, rep or dock: the amber fades out in 200ms.
- **Never blocks the app.** The app renders and loads underneath from the first moment. A hard cap removes the overlay even if JavaScript never runs.

### Out of Scope
- Android custom splash. It is generated by the OS from the manifest; `background_color` stays `#f2b544`, matching the launch frame.
- iPad and landscape launch images (the manifest is portrait-only and the target is the iPhone).
- Gating the splash on data readiness (that was option D, not chosen).

## Capabilities
### Modified Capabilities
- `app-shell`: ADDED "The installed app opens with a branded launch".

## Approach
Approach 1 from the exploration:
- Static launch images.
- A CSS-first overlay. The rise and rep phases are pure CSS from first paint.
- A small client container. After hydration it measures the dock target, sets CSS variables, starts the contract-and-dock phase, and removes the overlay at the end.

## Risks
- **iOS launch-image matching is exact.** An iPhone size without a matching image shows a blank launch. Mitigation: cover all current sizes, and keep the list in one table in the generator.
- **Hydration later than 0.95s.** The dock waits for hydration. If hydration has not happened by 3s, a CSS-only fallback fades the overlay.
- **Overlay over the status bar.** The status bar is `black-translucent` and the overlay is `fixed inset-0`, so it covers the status bar area exactly like the launch image.

## Owner decisions
- Design variant: **E**, Rise and dock (Ian, 2026-10-08).
- Defaults the orchestrator chose; Ian can override them at review:
  - standalone only;
  - every cold load;
  - reduced motion becomes a 200ms fade;
  - a 3s hard cap;
  - no tab bar → fade in place.
